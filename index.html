Rule 2202 support

ZEV/EV corridor data infrastructure supporting Rule 2202 AVR workflows.

Relay Rider can help employers and their certified Employee Transportation Coordinators organize zero-emission commute participation data, validate commute records, analyze corridor and EV signals, and prepare review-ready inputs for Rule 2202 workflows.

Important: Relay Rider is a software and data-support tool. It may provide future administrative support, but it is not the compliance authority, does not certify employer compliance, and does not replace the employer's certified ETC or applicable South Coast AQMD filing requirements.

Organization discovery

Research beta by design.

Relay Rider is developing institution-sponsored commuter research infrastructure with explicit product, safety, privacy, and compliance boundaries.

Does Relay Rider guarantee transportation?

No. Relay Rider is a research beta coordination platform. It does not guarantee a route match, participant placement, incentive, or program placement.

Is Relay Rider a Rule 2202 compliance authority?

No. Relay Rider may provide future administrative support for Rule 2202-related workflows, but it is not a compliance authority or certification service. Employers and their ETCs remain responsible for applicable determinations, filings, and program requirements.

Is Green Wallet a cash or stored-value payment?

No. Green Wallet refers to institution-sponsored Green Route Credit only, and any future support depends on sponsor approval, eligibility rules, and administrative decision-making.

Are the map locations active service points?

No. They are illustrative candidate concepts and are not approved service points, operating routes, or charger availability claims.

Does the intake collect an exact home address?

No. The public intake uses general geography such as ZIP code and destination area. Deeper information should only be requested when operationally necessary.
